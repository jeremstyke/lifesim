Frequently asked questions

Is Life Sim free?

Yes. Life Sim is free to play inside Telegram. An optional VIP pass and a few extras can be bought with Telegram Stars, but you can play a whole life without paying.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Life Sim is a Telegram Mini App: it opens instantly inside Telegram on iPhone, Android, desktop and web. You only need a Telegram account.

How long does one life take?

A few minutes if you rush, much longer if you manage every detail. You can start as a baby or jump straight to 6 or 18 years old.

Which languages are available?

English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi and Indonesian.

What happens when my character dies?

Your life earns a title and a life story you can share. Then you can start a brand new life, or continue the story as one of your children.

Who makes the stories?

Every year of your life is written by AI (Claude), based on your age, your job, your relationships and your past choices, so no two lives are the same.
