# Life Sim 🎂

**Live a whole life in a few minutes, right inside Telegram.** Grow up, study, fall in love, build a career or a criminal empire, and see where your choices take you.

👉 **Play now on Telegram: [@LsimgameBot](https://t.me/LsimgameBot/Play?startapp=src_github)**

## Features

- 🎂 **Age up year by year**: every year brings a new story written just for your life
- 🎯 **Life goals**: 3 random challenges per life, with rewards up to $50,000
- 📖 **Stories that remember you**: your past choices come back years later
- ✨ **Rare events**: fortune, fame or disaster can change everything
- 💼 **Career, studies, love, family, properties and crime**
- 🏆 **Your life in one title** at the end: The Godfather, The Tycoon, Living Legend...
- 🌍 **11 languages**

## How it is built

- **Telegram Mini App**: the game opens instantly inside Telegram, nothing to install
- **Claude AI**: writes the stories and events of every life
- **Backend**: Railway + Supabase
- **Payments**: Telegram Stars
