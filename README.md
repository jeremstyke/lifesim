## 🎮 How it works

Tap **🎂 Age up** and a new year of your life begins, with a story written just for you. Make a choice, or type your own move, and watch the consequences unfold.

| Age | What happens |
|:---:|---|
| 👶 **0** | You are born. Family, looks and luck are drawn. |
| 🧒 **6** | School starts: grades, friends, first bullies. |
| 🧑 **18** | Adulthood: study, work, or take the risky road. |
| 💼 **30** | Career, love, a house, maybe kids. |
| 👨 **50** | Fortune, scandals, second chances. |
| 👴 **90** | Your legacy, and the title your life earned. |
